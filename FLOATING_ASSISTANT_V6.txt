JARVIS V6 Floating Assistant

- Tiny 38dp floating J button over all apps.
- Tap: same-size mic icon appears and Google/Android speech recognition listens.
- After command: mic returns to J.
- Drag bubble anywhere. Position is remembered.
- Universal installed-app opener: say app name + open/kholo.
- YouTube/Google search, camera, WhatsApp, Chrome, ChatGPT, Home and torch on/off.
- Requires Display over other apps permission and microphone permission.
- Foreground notification keeps the assistant active more reliably.
