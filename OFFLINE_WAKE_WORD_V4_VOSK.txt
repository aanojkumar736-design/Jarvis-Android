JARVIS V4 - Vosk Offline Wake Mode

No account, no AccessKey, no login.
First start downloads the official small English Vosk model once (~40 MB).
After that wake detection works offline.

Commands:
- Jarvis
- Jarvis light on
- Jarvis light off
- Say Jarvis, wait for reply, then say light on/off

Vivo: allow microphone, notifications, background activity, auto-start, and unrestricted battery.
